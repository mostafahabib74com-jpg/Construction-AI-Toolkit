# Commercial integrity

- Import price and quantity data only from approved Estimation, QS, Commercial, Finance, Tax, and Contracts artifacts.
- Reconcile cover letter, price summary, BOQ, schedules, forms, options, alternates, discounts, currencies, taxes, duties, escalation, provisional sums, and portal values.
- Distinguish blank, zero, included, excluded, not applicable, not offered, and provisional.
- Keep assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, deviations, clarifications, and commercial terms in separate controlled registers.
- Prevent technical and commercial volumes from contradicting scope, schedule, responsibilities, or stated qualifications.
- Do not expose confidential estimate build-ups where the tender requests only summary pricing.
- Do not commit a price, alter an approved total, accept a term, or issue a commercial position.
