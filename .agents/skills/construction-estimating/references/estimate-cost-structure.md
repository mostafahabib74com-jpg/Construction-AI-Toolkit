# Construction Estimate Cost Structure

Use this reference with the `construction-estimating` skill.

## Recommended cost buckets
1. Direct measured works
2. Direct non-measured / lump-sum works
3. Temporary works
4. Project preliminaries/site indirects
5. Procurement/logistics additions
6. Risk/contingency
7. Corporate overhead
8. Profit
9. Financing/commercial adjustments
10. Taxes/statutory additions where applicable

## Status coding
Use one clear status for every major estimate input:
- **V** = Verified
- **A** = Assumed
- **P** = Provisional
- **Q** = Quotation pending
- **E** = Excluded

## Estimate bridge
A clear bid-price bridge should allow management to see how the price develops from cost to selling price without mixing categories.

## Sensitivity
Prioritize sensitivity testing for:
- high-value quantities;
- volatile materials;
- low-productivity activities;
- long-duration indirects;
- major subcontract packages;
- uncertain risk allowances;
- payment/financing assumptions.
