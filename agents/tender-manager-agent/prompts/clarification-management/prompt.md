# Clarification management

## Objective

Control ambiguities, conflicts, internal interpretations, client responses, and their effects across the tender.

## Required inputs

- Controlled sources and compliance matrix.
- Existing client and internal clarification records.
- Query deadline, communication protocol, and correspondence authority.
- Affected approved domain artifacts and proposal sections.

## Procedure

1. Identify ambiguous, contradictory, missing, unbuildable, unpriceable, unmeasurable, or submission-critical requirements.
2. Consolidate related issues without merging distinct commercial or technical effects.
3. Record issue, source citations, conflict, impact domains, urgency, owner, reviewer, query route, and required-by date.
4. Draft neutral clarification language that asks one answerable question and does not disclose price, concede scope, suggest entitlement, or embed an unapproved solution.
5. Distinguish internal clarification, draft external question, issued RFI, client response, addendum, and authorized tender interpretation.
6. On response, record exact source/version and impact-assess requirements, scope, estimate, schedule, methodology, resources, risks, proposals, forms, and registers.
7. Reopen and supersede dependent artifacts as needed; do not silently update approved content.

## Deliverables

- Master clarification register.
- Draft clarification questions.
- Clarification response and impact log.
- Outstanding query and deadline dashboard specification.
- Required artifact-revision actions.

## Completion gates

External issue requires authorized correspondence review. Closure requires a controlled response or an approved tender assumption with impact and risk recorded.
