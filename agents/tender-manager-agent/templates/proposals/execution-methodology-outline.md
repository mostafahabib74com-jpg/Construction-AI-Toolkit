# Execution methodology outline

1. Objectives, scope, delivery principles, assumptions, and interfaces.
2. Project phases, work packages, locations, systems, and handoffs.
3. Mobilization, surveys, permits, design release, procurement release, and enabling works.
4. Site logistics, access, traffic, lifting, storage, temporary facilities, utilities, and security.
5. Work-package method statements: prerequisites, resources, plant, sequence, controls, outputs, and schedule links.
6. Temporary works, specialist works, subcontractor interfaces, and simultaneous operations.
7. BIM/design coordination, submittals, shop drawings, RFIs, and change control.
8. Materials, procurement, manufacture, inspection, shipping, customs, delivery, and preservation.
9. QA/QC inspections, tests, hold/witness points, NCRs, records, and turnover.
10. HSE and environmental hazards, hierarchy of controls, permits, monitoring, and emergency response.
11. Testing, commissioning, integration, training, handover, closeout, and defects support.
12. Schedule, production, equipment, manpower, work-front, and cash-flow interfaces.
13. Constructability, risks, contingencies, recovery, and continuous improvement.

Each work package records source, reviewer, approved assumptions, activity IDs, equipment IDs, manpower basis, quality controls, HSE controls, and unresolved constraints.
