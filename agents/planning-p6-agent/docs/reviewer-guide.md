# Reviewer guide

## Planning Manager

Confirm scope coverage, WBS, activity definition, durations, logic, calendars, constraints, coding, milestones, schedule settings, critical/longest path, float, progress, and change control.

## Construction Manager

Confirm methodology, sequence, access, productivity, temporary works, interfaces, logistics, and recovery feasibility.

## Procurement and Design Managers

Confirm package strategy, lead times, approval cycles, resubmissions, long-lead exposure, information release, and required-on-site dates.

## Project Controls and Cost Control

Confirm resource/cost mapping, curves, periodization, cash-flow logic, totals, earned-value basis, and reporting rules.

## Contract and Claims Manager

Confirm contemporaneous schedule selection, event chronology, notices, method suitability, concurrency approach, and separation of schedule facts from entitlement.

Reviewers must reject invented actuals, unsupported durations, hidden constraints, manipulated logic, unverified CPM output, or unexplained date/float/cost differences.
