# Commercial proposal outline

Use client forms and ordering as authoritative.

1. Commercial cover and authorized offer letter.
2. Price summary by required currency and price base date.
3. Completed BOQ, schedule of prices, rate schedules, and provisional sums.
4. Alternates, options, value-engineering proposals, and discounts with exact conditions.
5. Taxes, duties, customs, exchange, escalation, indexation, and withholding treatment.
6. Payment terms, advance, retention, milestones, validity, bonds, guarantees, and insurance.
7. Commercial assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, and deviations in prescribed forms.
8. Tender forms, declarations, securities, powers of attorney, and signatures.
9. Commercial reconciliation and approval certificate.
10. Portal-field and envelope mapping.

Every total identifies its approved source artifact and version. Blank, zero, included, excluded, not applicable, not offered, and provisional values remain distinct.
