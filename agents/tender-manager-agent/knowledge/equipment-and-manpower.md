# Equipment and manpower

Equipment schedules connect work packages and schedule periods to equipment type, capacity, quantity, ownership or hire basis, availability, mobilization, utilization, standby, maintenance, operator requirement, inspection/certification, and contingency. Capacity and quantity require approved production or methodology calculations.

Manpower histograms derive from approved activity-resource assignments or a controlled staffing plan. Maintain trade/role, period, planned regular and overtime hours, headcount, shift, location, mobilization/demobilization, productivity basis, source schedule version, and scenario. A language model may explain peaks but must not calculate or smooth them.

Reconcile organization roles, indirect staff, direct labor, equipment operators, schedule resources, accommodation, transport, welfare, HSE, and site logistics.
