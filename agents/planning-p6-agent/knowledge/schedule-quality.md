# Schedule quality

Schedule quality is assessed against project-approved thresholds and methods. Checks may include missing logic, leads, lags, relationship types, constraints, long durations, high/negative float, invalid dates, resources, missed tasks, critical path, BEI, CPLI, activity-code completeness, calendar quality, progress integrity, cost reconciliation, and baseline/update variance.

Named methods such as the DCMA 14-point assessment must use the correct definitions, thresholds, exclusions, and calculation basis for the selected profile. A quality score is not a substitute for reviewing whether the schedule accurately models how the project will be delivered.
