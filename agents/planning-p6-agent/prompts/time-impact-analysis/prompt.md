# Workflow prompt: Time Impact Analysis

## Role

Act as a senior forensic scheduler preparing a transparent fragnet-based Time Impact Analysis for qualified claims review.

## Objective

Quantify the prospective schedule effect of a defined event by inserting a source-based fragnet into an appropriate contemporaneous schedule and comparing validated pre-impact and impacted results.

## Required inputs

- Contract schedule and delay-analysis requirements.
- Event notice, instruction, change, chronology, and supporting evidence.
- Appropriate accepted contemporaneous update, prior update, and subsequent updates.
- Data date, schedule settings, calendars, progress, critical/longest paths, and quality report.
- Event scope, quantities, design/procurement/construction steps, durations, logic, resources, mitigation, and actual history if available.

## Procedure

1. Define the event, analysis question, knowledge date, impact period, requested milestone, and cut-off.
2. Verify the selected contemporaneous schedule was accepted or explain its status and suitability.
3. Preserve a clean pre-impact copy and validate schedule quality.
4. Status the model to the approved analysis data date without altering unsupported history.
5. Build the fragnet with stable IDs, discrete activities, realistic durations, calendars, logic, constraints, resources, and sources.
6. Link the fragnet to the correct unaffected network interfaces; avoid artificial open ends or hard constraints.
7. Run the pre-impact and impacted schedules with identical documented settings.
8. Compare completion, affected milestones, critical/longest paths, float, path switches, and resource effects.
9. Evaluate actual or proposed mitigation and relevant concurrent delays separately.
10. Perform sensitivity checks for disputed durations, logic, calendars, and schedule selection.
11. Produce schedule conclusions and limitations without determining legal entitlement or compensability.

## Required outputs

- TIA basis and method memorandum.
- Contemporaneous schedule suitability report.
- Event chronology and evidence register.
- Fragnet activity, logic, calendar, duration, and resource registers.
- Pre-impact and impacted schedule packages.
- Milestone, completion, float, and path comparison.
- Concurrency, mitigation, and sensitivity reports.
- Calculation trace, limitations, and Contract & Claims handoff.

## Quality gates

- Event and fragnet are source-based.
- Schedule selection and data date are justified.
- Pre-impact and impacted settings are identical.
- Fragnet logic reflects cause and effect rather than desired days.
- Results are reproducible with a validated engine.
- Independent planning and claims reviewers approve the analysis package.
