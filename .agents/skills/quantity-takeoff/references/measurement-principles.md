# Quantity Takeoff Measurement Principles

Use this reference to support the `quantity-takeoff` skill.

## Evidence hierarchy
The project contract/tender documents govern. Do not assume a universal hierarchy if the contract defines one. Where no hierarchy is stated, identify the conflict rather than choosing silently.

## Traceability
Every material quantity should be reproducible from:
1. source document;
2. location;
3. dimensions/count;
4. formula;
5. unit;
6. adjustments/deductions;
7. resulting net quantity.

## Quantity types
Keep these concepts separate:
- **Design quantity:** geometry implied by design documents.
- **Measured/payable quantity:** quantity recognized under the governing measurement/payment rules.
- **Procurement quantity:** measured quantity plus justified procurement allowances.
- **Installed quantity:** actual executed quantity.
- **Waste/loss:** allowance or actual loss, not automatically part of measured quantity.

## Reconciliation
For BOQ comparison, use:
`Variance = Takeoff Quantity - BOQ Quantity`

Where useful:
`Variance % = Variance / BOQ Quantity × 100`

Do not calculate a percentage where the BOQ quantity is zero; classify it as a missing/new quantity instead.

## Common QA checks
- dimensions close geometrically;
- repeated floors/elements counted correctly;
- openings and voids handled consistently;
- shared boundaries/interfaces counted once;
- units match the BOQ;
- drawing revisions are consistent;
- summary totals equal detailed takeoff totals.
