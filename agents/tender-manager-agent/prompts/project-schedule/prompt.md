# Tender project schedule

## Objective

Coordinate a compliant tender schedule package from an approved Planning & Primavera P6 Agent artifact and align it with the complete proposal.

## Required inputs

- Contract dates, milestones, phasing, submission requirements, and schedule specification.
- Approved Planning Agent schedule, basis, narrative, calendars, WBS, activities, logic, constraints, resources, and QA report.
- Methodology, procurement, material submittal, shop drawing, equipment, manpower, commissioning, and handover inputs.
- Approved assumptions, exclusions, clarifications, and risks.

## Procedure

1. Verify the owning Planning Agent, workflow, schedule ID/version, data date, status, approval, and artifact checksum.
2. Map every schedule requirement and milestone to the controlled schedule and proposal response.
3. Confirm alignment among WBS, methodology, work fronts, design, procurement, submittals, equipment, manpower, testing, commissioning, handover, and contractual dates.
4. Use deterministic schedule tools for dates, float, critical path, histograms, curves, and quality metrics; do not reproduce calculations in prose.
5. Prepare required views, layouts, narratives, milestone schedules, calendars, resource summaries, and electronic file references.
6. Identify any proposal statement or commercial term that conflicts with the schedule.
7. Preserve the approved schedule; proposal formatting creates controlled derivatives only.

## Deliverables

- Tender schedule package index.
- Milestone and phase schedule.
- Schedule narrative and basis cross-reference.
- Required P6/PDF/tabular/graphic deliverable specification.
- Proposal-method-resource-procurement reconciliation.
- Schedule compliance and exception report.

## Completion gates

Planning Manager approves every schedule derivative. Tender Manager confirms package compliance. The agent cannot generate or validate native P6 files without approved tooling.
