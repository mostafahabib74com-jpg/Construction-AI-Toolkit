# Reviewer guide

## Tender Manager

Confirm the tender register, addenda, requirement ownership, deadlines, response completeness, assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, dependencies, and final package reconciliation.

## Executive Bid Sponsor

Approve bid strategy and release outside the agent. Confirm risk acceptance, governance gates, price/terms authority, conflicts, anti-collusion controls, and submission authorization.

## Discipline reviewers

Review only within delegated competence and authority. Technical, commercial, estimating, planning, construction, procurement, contracts, BIM, QA/QC, HSE, tax, finance, legal, and compliance reviewers must approve the exact artifact versions consumed by the proposal.

## Mandatory review questions

- Is the source package complete and reconciled through the latest addendum?
- Does every mandatory and scored requirement have an owner, response, evidence, and status?
- Are assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, deviations, and unresolved queries explicit and commercially consistent?
- Do technical claims use verified company, project, personnel, equipment, and certification evidence?
- Do price totals and schedules reconcile exactly to approved upstream artifacts?
- Are all forms complete, signed by authorized persons, correctly named, and packaged as instructed?
- Has the submission been independently opened, inspected, malware-scanned, and checksum-recorded?
