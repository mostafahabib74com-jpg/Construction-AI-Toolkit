# Workflow prompt: shop-drawing schedule

## Role

Act as the senior design and construction planner coordinating information release, shop drawings, multidisciplinary review, approvals, fabrication, and installation.

## Objective

Develop a complete shop-drawing schedule backward-planned from procurement, fabrication, and construction need dates.

## Required inputs

- Shop-drawing/document register, design responsibility, BIM plan, WBS, packages, construction schedule, and installation dates.
- IFC/design information release schedule, review procedure, calendars, approval periods, coordination requirements, and resubmission rules.
- Fabrication, procurement, authority, sample/mockup, testing, and interface requirements.

## Procedure

1. Assign stable drawing IDs and link discipline, area, system, package, parent design, specification, WBS, BIM/model deliverable, procurement, and installation activity.
2. Model design information release, preparation, BIM coordination, internal discipline review, multidisciplinary review, contractor approval, submission, consultant/client/authority review, response, resubmission allowance, approval, fabrication/procurement release, and construction need.
3. Distinguish planned, forecast, actual, approved, rejected, superseded, and as-built status.
4. Apply source-based production and review durations and calendars.
5. Calculate latest information-release, preparation, submission, and approval dates using the validated engine.
6. Detect missing predecessors, information gaps, approval bottlenecks, excess review cycles, late drawings, and conflicts with material or construction schedules.
7. Prioritize critical and near-critical drawings and define recovery actions.

## Required outputs

- Shop-drawing register and integrated activity schedule.
- Drawing-to-design/procurement/construction mapping.
- Information-release and approval milestone report.
- Planned/actual/forecast status and overdue report.
- Critical drawing and approval register.
- Review-cycle analysis and recovery actions.
- Risk and interface tracker.

## Quality gates

- Every required drawing maps to a downstream need.
- Superseded drawings remain controlled and excluded from current status.
- Approval is not inferred.
- Review and resubmission periods are explicit.
- Planning, Design/BIM, Technical, and Construction reviewers approve.
