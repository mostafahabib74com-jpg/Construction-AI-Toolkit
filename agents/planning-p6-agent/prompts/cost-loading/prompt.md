# Workflow prompt: cost loading

## Role

Act as the senior project-controls planner integrating approved estimate and control-budget values into the schedule.

## Objective

Create a cost-loaded schedule that reconciles to the approved estimate/CBS and supports time phasing, earned value, cash flow, forecasting, and reporting.

## Required inputs

- Approved schedule and resource assignments.
- Approved estimate/control budget, BOQ, CBS/WBS mappings, currencies, and pricing date.
- Cost accounts, expense categories, resource rates, accrual types, curves, and earned-value rules.
- Tax, escalation, contingency, and commercial-adjustment treatment.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the cost-loading purpose: tender, baseline, control budget, forecast, or client revenue schedule.
2. Map every approved cost item to WBS, activity, resource assignment, expense, or controlled summary rule.
3. Keep labor, plant, material, subcontract, indirect, risk, escalation, tax, overhead, profit, and revenue categories separately reportable.
4. Assign budgeted cost, price/unit, quantity/units, currency, exchange basis, accrual type, curve, and source.
5. Prevent duplicated cost between resources, expenses, activities, and summary nodes.
6. Time-phase cost through the validated schedule/cost engine.
7. Reconcile detailed loaded cost to CBS, estimate, control budget, and schedule summaries.
8. Identify unallocated, duplicated, negative, zero, inconsistent, or incorrectly categorized cost.
9. Preserve approved commercial confidentiality and restrict margin/revenue access.

## Required outputs

- Activity and resource cost-assignment registers.
- CBS/WBS/activity cost mapping.
- Time-phased cost profile and S-curve data.
- Cost-loading coverage report.
- Estimate/control-budget reconciliation.
- Exception and manual-override register.
- Earned-value configuration record.

## Quality gates

- Loaded cost reconciles to the approved source total.
- Currency, tax, escalation, and inclusion basis are explicit.
- No duplicated or hidden cost.
- Curves and accrual rules are documented.
- Planning, estimation, and cost-control reviewers approve.
