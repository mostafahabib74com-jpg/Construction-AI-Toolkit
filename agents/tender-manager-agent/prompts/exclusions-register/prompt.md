# Tender exclusions register

## Objective

Create an explicit, defensible, source-linked exclusion register without concealing noncompliance or contradicting the tender offer.

## Required inputs

- Controlled scope, BOQ, requirements, contract, estimate, assumptions, queries, and proposal artifacts.
- Approved commercial strategy and authority limits.
- Client rules governing qualifications, deviations, and exclusions.

## Procedure

1. Identify scope, supply, service, risk, quantity, price, schedule, permit, tax, utility, interface, temporary-work, test, and third-party items not included in the approved offer.
2. Distinguish true exclusions from assumptions, qualifications, deviations, optional items, client-supplied items, provisional sums, and scope gaps.
3. State each exclusion precisely, including boundaries, interfaces, affected requirements and BOQ items, responsible party where sourced, and commercial/time implications.
4. Check whether the tender prohibits exclusions or requires deviations in a prescribed form.
5. Reconcile every exclusion to estimate, BOQ, schedule, methodology, responsibility matrix, technical proposal, commercial proposal, and forms.
6. Flag exclusions that create mandatory noncompliance, unsafe or unbuildable interfaces, hidden scope gaps, or unacceptable ambiguity.
7. Record reviewer, approval reference, disclosure location, and supersession history.

## Deliverables

- Exclusions register.
- Exclusion-versus-assumption/qualification/deviation reconciliation.
- Scope and BOQ cross-reference.
- Compliance and risk impact report.
- Client-disclosure schedule.

## Completion gates

Commercial and Contracts reviewers must approve every disclosed exclusion. The Tender Manager must confirm consistency across the complete submission.
