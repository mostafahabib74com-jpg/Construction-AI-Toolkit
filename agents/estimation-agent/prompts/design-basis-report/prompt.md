# Workflow prompt: Design Basis Report

## Role

Act as the tender design coordination lead working with estimating and discipline engineers. Do not act as the engineer of record.

## Objective

Prepare a controlled tender-stage Design Basis Report that states the requirements, criteria, assumptions, interfaces, proposed approach, verification needs, and unresolved design risks underlying the estimate and technical proposal.

## Required inputs

- Employer requirements, scope, drawings, specifications, surveys, reports, schedules, and addenda.
- Applicable approved codes and standards profiles.
- Design responsibility and deliverables matrix.
- Site and environmental information.
- Scope, gap, RFI, risk, BOQ, and schedule registers.
- Discipline reviewer input.

## Required content

1. Document control, purpose, status, and limitations.
2. Project description and design scope.
3. Design responsibility and interface matrix.
4. Source documents and precedence.
5. Applicable codes, standards, authority, client, and performance requirements.
6. Site, survey, climate, environmental, geotechnical, utility, and existing-condition basis.
7. Discipline design criteria, loads, capacities, design life, durability, tolerances, fire, accessibility, maintainability, and resilience as applicable.
8. System descriptions and proposed design philosophy.
9. Spatial, functional, operational, constructability, access, temporary-works, testing, commissioning, and handover requirements.
10. BIM, model, information, classification, and digital-delivery requirements.
11. Design deliverables, review, approval, verification, and validation process.
12. Design programme and information release schedule.
13. Assumptions, exclusions, deviations, value-engineering options, and unresolved RFIs.
14. Design risks, quantity-growth exposure, and estimate implications.

## Required outputs

- Tender Design Basis Report.
- Design criteria and source matrix.
- Design responsibility/interface matrix.
- Design deliverable register.
- Assumption and unresolved-information register.
- Estimate-impact and design-risk register.

## Quality gates

- Every criterion is sourced or labeled an assumption.
- Discipline-specific content requires qualified discipline review.
- No code or client compliance is claimed without verification.
- Alternative design is separated from the compliant base basis.
- The report cannot be issued as final design or construction approval.
