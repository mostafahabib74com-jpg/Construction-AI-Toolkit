# Procurement, material submittal, and shop drawing schedules

Each package or deliverable should model the complete lead-time chain rather than a single delivery milestone.

## Procurement chain

Requirements definition, RFQ, bidder period, technical/commercial evaluation, approvals, purchase order, design/shop drawing, material submittal, sample/mockup, manufacture, inspection/FAT, release, shipping, customs, delivery, receiving, storage, and required-on-site date.

## Material submittal chain

Preparation, internal review, submission, consultant/client review, resubmission allowance, approval, procurement release, manufacture, delivery, inspection, and installation need date.

## Shop drawing chain

Design information release, preparation, multidisciplinary coordination, internal review, submission, review, resubmission allowance, approval, fabrication/procurement release, and construction need date.

Activities must be backward planned from approved need dates, linked to design and construction, and updated from controlled registers.
