# Pricing risk taxonomy

Pricing risk shall be assessed across:

- Scope gaps, overlaps, interfaces, and design development.
- Quantity uncertainty and measurement-rule ambiguity.
- Rate validity, quotation coverage, exclusions, and price expiry.
- Labor availability, productivity, overtime, shifts, accommodation, and transport.
- Material availability, lead time, escalation, waste, freight, storage, and customs.
- Plant availability, utilization, standby, mobilization, maintenance, and fuel.
- Subcontractor solvency, capacity, qualifications, exclusions, and bid spread.
- Programme duration, phasing, access, weather, interfaces, and acceleration.
- Ground conditions, utilities, existing assets, and site information.
- Design responsibility, approvals, permits, testing, commissioning, and handover.
- Currency, inflation, taxes, duties, payment delay, retention, and financing.
- Bonds, insurance, liquidated damages, warranties, indemnities, and liabilities.
- Procurement strategy, single-source exposure, long-lead items, and nominated parties.
- Market competition, strategic pricing, alternative bids, and client behavior.

Every risk records cause, event, effect, source, owner, probability, cost impact range, schedule impact, response, residual exposure, pricing treatment, and approval status. Risk allowance must not be double counted with contingency, escalation, or embedded rate allowances.
