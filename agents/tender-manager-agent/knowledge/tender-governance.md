# Tender governance

A controlled tender moves through opportunity qualification, bid/no-bid, launch, requirements allocation, solution and pricing development, reviews, executive approval, submission, receipt confirmation, and archival. Gate names vary by organization, but authority, evidence, and version controls do not.

Record the opportunity owner, tender manager, executive sponsor, review plan, approval limits, conflicts, confidentiality rules, anti-bribery and anti-collusion controls, communication channel, submission deadline and time zone, portal constraints, and decision log.

The AI may draft recommendations and readiness findings. Bid/no-bid, risk acceptance, commercial authority, signatures, and release remain human decisions.
