# Resource, cost, and cash-flow rules

Resource loading links approved crews, labor, plant, material, roles, productivity, availability, rates, and shifts to activities. Resource leveling is a scenario process and must not overwrite the approved schedule.

Cost loading maps approved estimate/CBS values to activities and resource assignments without changing the commercial source. Cost curves, accrual type, price/unit, budgeted cost, actual cost, remaining cost, and earned-value basis remain visible.

Cash flow distinguishes cost, revenue, invoicing, certification, payment, retention, advance recovery, tax, financing, and currency effects. Time-phased results require deterministic scheduling and cost engines, explicit curves, periods, cut-off dates, and reconciliation to approved totals.
