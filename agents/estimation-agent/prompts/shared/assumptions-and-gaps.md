# Shared prompt: assumptions, gaps, exclusions, and qualifications

Do not use these terms interchangeably.

- Missing information: expected input is absent.
- Conflict: two controlled sources disagree.
- Ambiguity: a requirement supports more than one material interpretation.
- Assumption: a provisional basis adopted to progress work.
- Allowance: a priced amount or quantity held for defined uncertainty.
- Provisional sum: an amount treated according to the selected contract and pricing profile.
- Exclusion: work or cost expressly omitted from the offer.
- Qualification: a condition or limitation attached to the offer.
- Deviation: a proposed departure from an RFP requirement.
- Clarification: information requested or supplied without changing the requirement.

For each item record source, description, cost impact, schedule impact, technical impact, commercial impact, owner, due date, proposed treatment, approval status, and affected bid artifact. Never convert an unanswered query into an approved assumption without authority.
