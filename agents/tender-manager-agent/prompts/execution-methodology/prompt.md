# Execution methodology

## Objective

Develop a constructible, schedule-aligned, resource-supported execution methodology that responds directly to the tender requirements.

## Required inputs

- Controlled scope, specifications, drawings, design basis, site information, milestones, constraints, and interfaces.
- Approved construction strategy, WBS, tender schedule, logistics, procurement, resources, temporary works, BIM, QA/QC, HSE, environmental, testing, and commissioning inputs.
- Clarifications, assumptions, exclusions, risks, and client method requirements.

## Procedure

1. Define delivery phases, work packages, locations, systems, and interface boundaries.
2. For each work package document prerequisites, inputs, sequence, labor, equipment, materials, logistics, temporary works, permits, inspections/tests, hold points, HSE/environment controls, outputs, and handoffs.
3. Link sequence to approved schedule activities and procurement/design release points.
4. Reconcile production assumptions and capacity to equipment and manpower plans through deterministic calculations.
5. Address mobilization, site establishment, access, traffic, utilities, lifting, storage, work fronts, simultaneous operations, weather, security, commissioning, handover, and demobilization where applicable.
6. Identify constructability gaps, unsafe interfaces, incomplete design, missing permits, specialist dependencies, and contingency actions.
7. Maintain source and responsible-reviewer traceability for every material method.

## Deliverables

- Execution strategy and phased methodology.
- Work-package method matrix.
- Logistics and interface narrative/specification.
- Schedule-resource-method reconciliation.
- Constructability, risk, and contingency register.
- Methodology review and approval tracker.

## Completion gates

Require Construction, Planning, Procurement, Design/BIM, QA/QC, HSE, and Tender reviews as applicable. Unverified feasibility blocks approval.
