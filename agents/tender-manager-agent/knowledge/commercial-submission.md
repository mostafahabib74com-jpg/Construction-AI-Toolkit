# Commercial submission

The commercial proposal assembles only approved Estimation, QS, Commercial, Contracts, Finance, and Tax artifacts. Preserve estimate ID/version, currency, price base date, exchange rates, taxes, duties, escalation, provisional sums, alternatives, options, discounts, bonds, insurance, payment terms, validity, qualifications, assumptions, and exclusions.

Reconcile summary, BOQ, schedules, forms, alternates, and electronic portal values. Blank, zero, included, excluded, not applicable, and not offered are distinct. Never infer a commercial position from silence.

Assumptions describe the basis used to prepare the offer. Exclusions state scope not included. Qualifications modify or clarify tender obligations. Deviations identify noncompliance. Keep them distinct and obtain explicit commercial/contract review.
