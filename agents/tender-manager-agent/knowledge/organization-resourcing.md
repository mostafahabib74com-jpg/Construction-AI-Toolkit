# Organization and responsibility

An organization chart represents the proposed governance and delivery model, not merely a list of names. Define reporting lines, functional interfaces, site/home-office split, phase deployment, authority, responsibility, escalation, deputies, and client interfaces.

Personnel names, titles, CVs, availability, mobilization dates, certifications, licenses, and commitments require verified sources and permission. Do not fabricate candidates, modify credentials, or expose personal data beyond tender requirements.

Provide a role dictionary and RACI or responsibility matrix with the chart specification. Flag vacancies, shared resources, conditional appointments, and approval dependencies.
