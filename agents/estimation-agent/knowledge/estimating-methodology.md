# Estimating methodology

## Estimate lifecycle

The agent supports conceptual, feasibility, budget, definitive, tender, control, and change estimates. The estimate class must be selected explicitly and aligned with design maturity, intended use, expected accuracy range, available information, and approval authority.

## Mandatory estimate basis

Every estimate shall document:

- Purpose and estimate class.
- Project location, delivery strategy, scope, and design maturity.
- Source-document register and revision cut-off.
- Measurement and pricing basis.
- Currency, exchange rates, pricing date, tax, duties, and escalation.
- Work calendar, schedule, procurement, logistics, and phasing assumptions.
- Direct costs: labor, materials, plant, subcontract, and temporary works.
- Indirect costs: site preliminaries, supervision, facilities, utilities, testing, mobilization, demobilization, and closeout.
- Corporate overhead, risk allowance, contingency, escalation, profit, bonds, insurance, and other commercial adjustments.
- Assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, allowances, provisional sums, and unresolved queries.
- Reconciliation to previous estimate, budget, BOQ, quotations, and bid total.

## Calculation rules

Quantities, rates, productivity, resource hours, extensions, markups, subtotals, taxes, escalation, contingency, and currency conversions must be calculated deterministically. AI may propose a formula, mapping, or assumption but must not claim an unverified arithmetic result.

## Estimate maturity

The result shall state whether information is measured, quoted, calculated, benchmarked, assumed, provisional, or excluded. Confidence must consider scope definition, quantity maturity, quotation coverage, market volatility, schedule maturity, risk exposure, and data quality.

## Reconciliation

Reconciliation is mandatory between:

- RFP scope and BOQ coverage.
- BOQ and CBS.
- Estimate and quotations.
- Estimate and schedule or procurement allowances.
- Detailed sheets and executive summary.
- Base cost and final bid price.
- Current and previous estimate versions.

Any unexplained variance blocks final bid readiness.
