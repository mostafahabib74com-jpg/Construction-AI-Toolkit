# Tender evidence discipline

- Cite the controlled source ID, revision, and precise page, clause, table, sheet/cell, drawing, portal field, or artifact reference.
- Quote only the minimum necessary text and preserve its meaning and context.
- Never cite generated proposal content as proof of a tender requirement or company capability.
- Mark OCR-derived evidence and extraction confidence.
- Distinguish client sources, company-approved sources, approved domain artifacts, user statements, assumptions, and recommendations.
- If evidence is ambiguous or contradictory, record all relevant sources and raise a clarification rather than selecting a favorable interpretation.
