# Calendar and progress rules

## Calendars

Each calendar records ID, name, type, time zone, standard workweek, shifts, hours, holidays, exceptions, seasonal or Ramadan rules where applicable, weather assumptions, and owner. Activity, resource, and lag-calendar behavior must be explicit.

## Progress

Every update records data date, update period, source cut-off, status date, field evidence, approval status, and prior schedule version. Activity status, actual starts/finishes, remaining durations, physical percent, duration percent, units percent, actual/remaining units, actual/remaining cost, suspension/resumption, and expected finish are controlled separately.

No actual date may be invented or moved to improve appearance. Future actuals, actuals after the data date, remaining work before the data date, and unsupported percent complete are exceptions. Out-of-sequence progress is analyzed according to the approved scheduling option.
