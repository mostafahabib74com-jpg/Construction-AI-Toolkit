# Tender assumptions register

## Objective

Create a controlled, impact-assessed register of the bases used where tender information is incomplete or a decision is required.

## Required inputs

- Compliance, clarification, RFI, scope-gap, risk, estimate, schedule, methodology, and commercial registers.
- Client responses and latest addenda.
- Organization assumption-approval authority.

## Procedure

1. Identify every material basis not established by a controlled source or approved artifact.
2. Write each assumption as a specific, testable statement; do not disguise exclusions, qualifications, deviations, or facts as assumptions.
3. Record reason, source gap, affected scope/WBS/BOQ/CBS/activity/section, technical effect, cost effect, time effect, risk, owner, reviewer, validity condition, and resolution route.
4. Check consistency across estimate, schedule, methodology, resources, technical proposal, commercial proposal, and forms.
5. Link assumptions to RFIs and replace them with controlled client answers when received, retaining history.
6. Prioritize assumptions by compliance, price, schedule, constructability, contract, safety, quality, and submission impact.
7. Separate internal estimating/planning assumptions from assumptions proposed for disclosure to the client.

## Deliverables

- Detailed assumptions register.
- Client-disclosure shortlist.
- Assumption impact and consistency matrix.
- Approval and supersession log.
- Critical unresolved assumption report.

## Completion gates

Commercially or contractually material assumptions require Commercial/Contracts approval. Safety, quality, design, or schedule assumptions require the corresponding professional review.
