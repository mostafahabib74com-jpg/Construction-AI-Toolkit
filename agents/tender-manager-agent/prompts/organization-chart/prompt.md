# Organization chart

## Objective

Define a credible tender organization chart, role dictionary, and responsibility model using approved personnel and deployment information.

## Required inputs

- Contractual organization and key-person requirements.
- Proposed governance, execution methodology, schedule phases, work locations, interfaces, and reporting rules.
- Approved personnel/CV records, availability, mobilization, employment or commitment status, certifications, and data-use permission.
- Company organization templates and client chart-format requirements.

## Procedure

1. Define project governance, client interfaces, reporting lines, functions, deputies, escalation, and home-office/site relationships.
2. Create stable position IDs independent of person names.
3. Record role purpose, responsibilities, authority, reporting line, phase, location, mobilization/demobilization, required competence, proposed person, availability, and evidence.
4. Flag vacancies, conditional appointments, shared resources, role conflicts, excessive spans of control, missing deputies, and unclear interfaces.
5. Create a RACI/responsibility matrix for major deliverables and decisions.
6. Reconcile named positions with indirect manpower, schedule resources, tender forms, CVs, and commercial allowances.
7. Specify chart nodes, edges, grouping, layout, legend, and version for a deterministic graphics renderer.
8. Minimize personal data and never invent or embellish credentials.

## Deliverables

- Organization chart data specification.
- Role and authority dictionary.
- Personnel evidence and availability register.
- RACI/responsibility matrix.
- Vacancy, conflict, and coverage report.
- Chart rendering specification.

## Completion gates

Require Project Director/Construction Manager, HR or authorized personnel-data owner, Tender Manager, and client-form review before issue.
