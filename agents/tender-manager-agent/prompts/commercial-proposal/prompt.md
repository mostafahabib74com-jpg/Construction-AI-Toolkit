# Commercial proposal

## Objective

Assemble a complete, internally reconciled commercial proposal from approved pricing and commercial artifacts without altering their controlled values.

## Required inputs

- Approved estimate and BOQ/price schedules with IDs, versions, currency, and price base date.
- Approved commercial strategy, contract review, tax, finance, bonds, insurance, validity, escalation, payment, and risk decisions.
- Client commercial forms, portal fields, alternates, options, discounts, and envelope rules.
- Approved assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, deviations, and clarifications.

## Procedure

1. Inventory all commercial deliverables, forms, schedules, declarations, securities, and approval requirements.
2. Import controlled totals without manual retyping where deterministic interfaces exist.
3. Reconcile estimate summary, BOQ, schedule of prices, alternates, options, discounts, taxes, duties, provisional sums, currencies, exchange rates, and portal values.
4. Distinguish blank, zero, included, excluded, not applicable, not offered, and provisional entries.
5. Align scope, responsibilities, quantities, schedule dates, methodology, qualifications, assumptions, exclusions, deviations, payment terms, validity, bonds, insurance, and commercial narrative.
6. Check arithmetic, rounding, formula, hidden-cell, unit, currency, tax, signature, and confidentiality controls using validated tools.
7. Produce a price change and approval trace; any changed upstream estimate version reopens reconciliation.

## Deliverables

- Commercial proposal outline and assembly specification.
- Price and form reconciliation report.
- Commercial schedules and portal-value mapping.
- Qualifications, assumptions, exclusions, and deviations schedule.
- Commercial risk and approval report.
- Final rendering and envelope specification.

## Completion gates

Require Estimation, Commercial, Finance/Tax, Contracts, Tender Manager, and authorized price approval as applicable. The agent cannot commit the offer.
