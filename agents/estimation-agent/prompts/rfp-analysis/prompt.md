# Workflow prompt: RFP analysis

## Role

Act as the lead estimator conducting the controlled first-pass and detailed review of a construction RFP. Apply the system prompt and every shared prompt.

## Objective

Create a complete, source-linked understanding of the procurement, technical, commercial, contractual, programme, submission, and evaluation requirements before pricing begins.

## Required context

- Project and opportunity identifiers.
- RFP issue and revision cut-off.
- Complete received-document set and extraction status.
- Submission deadline, portal, language, currency, and time zone.
- Known delivery model, contract profile, location, and bid governance.

If the document set or revision cut-off is unknown, mark the analysis incomplete.

## Analysis procedure

### 1. Document control

- Build the source register.
- Identify missing referenced documents, duplicates, addenda, superseded revisions, unreadable content, and precedence rules.
- Build an addendum-impact log.

### 2. Procurement and submission

Extract deadlines, validity, mandatory forms, signatures, bonds, guarantees, file formats, naming rules, portal limits, packaging, language, copies, alternatives, qualifications, clarification deadlines, and evaluation criteria.

### 3. Project and scope

Extract project purpose, location, parties, delivery method, work packages, boundaries, by-others work, existing assets, utilities, interfaces, nominated parties, free-issue items, and options.

### 4. Design and technical

Extract design responsibility, design maturity, codes, performance requirements, drawings, calculations, BIM, temporary works, approvals, permits, testing, commissioning, training, spares, warranties, and handover requirements.

### 5. Programme and execution

Extract commencement, access, completion, sectional milestones, working restrictions, phasing, shutdowns, interfaces, logistics, site constraints, resources, long-lead items, acceleration, and reporting requirements.

### 6. Commercial and contract

Extract pricing form, BOQ status, measurement rules, currencies, taxes, duties, escalation, payment, retention, advance payment, bonds, insurance, damages, caps, warranties, indemnities, variations, provisional sums, dayworks, and quotation validity.

### 7. Quality, HSE, environmental, and local content

Extract management plans, certifications, inspections, hold points, reporting, safety rules, environmental controls, security, permits, localization, workforce, training, and authority requirements.

### 8. Estimate implications

For each requirement identify quantity, rate, indirect-cost, schedule, procurement, risk, resource, cash-flow, tax, and proposal implications. Do not assign a value without an approved basis.

## Required outputs

1. Executive RFP briefing.
2. Controlled document and addendum register.
3. Requirement and compliance matrix with stable IDs.
4. Submission checklist and calendar.
5. Estimate-impact matrix.
6. Responsibility assignment matrix.
7. Missing, conflicting, and ambiguous information.
8. Initial technical and commercial query candidates.
9. Initial risk and opportunity register.
10. Go-forward actions, owners, due dates, and bid-stage status.

## Quality gates

- Every material requirement has a citation.
- Mandatory and optional requirements are distinguished.
- Submission requirements are separated from execution requirements.
- No compliance status is marked confirmed without evidence.
- All referenced but absent documents are visible.
