# Equipment schedule

## Objective

Develop a capacity-checked equipment schedule aligned with methodology, work fronts, project schedule, logistics, and approved availability.

## Required inputs

- Approved execution methodology and tender schedule.
- Work quantities, productivity/capacity basis, locations, shifts, and constraints.
- Approved company-owned and hired-equipment records, availability, rates, certification, transport, and maintenance requirements.
- HSE, lifting, environmental, access, and client equipment rules.

## Procedure

1. Define equipment categories, specifications, capacities, units, quantities, operators, attachments, and substitute criteria.
2. Link each item or fleet to work package, activity, period, location, shift, and production basis.
3. Calculate requirements, utilization, mobilization, standby, maintenance, and demobilization using deterministic tools.
4. Record ownership/hire basis, supplier or source, availability status, lead time, transport, permits, inspection, certification, fuel/power, and maintenance plan.
5. Identify peaks, double-booking, capacity gaps, incompatible access, certification gaps, and schedule risk.
6. Reconcile operators to manpower, equipment cost to estimate, and mobilization to logistics/procurement schedules.
7. Define primary and contingency equipment without making unsupported availability claims.

## Deliverables

- Detailed equipment schedule.
- Equipment-to-work-package/activity matrix.
- Capacity and utilization calculation references.
- Mobilization, certification, and maintenance register.
- Availability, conflict, and contingency report.
- Equipment summary for the technical proposal.

## Completion gates

Require Construction, Plant/Equipment, Planning, HSE, Estimation/Commercial, and Tender reviews. Unverified availability remains explicit.
