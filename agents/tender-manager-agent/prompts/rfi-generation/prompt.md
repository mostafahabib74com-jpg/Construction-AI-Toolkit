# Tender RFI generation

## Objective

Prepare concise, traceable, commercially safe tender RFIs for authorized review and issue.

## Required inputs

- Approved clarification candidates and source citations.
- Current compliance, scope-gap, technical, commercial, contract, quantity, and schedule findings.
- Client RFI form, numbering, channel, limit, and deadline rules.
- Responsible discipline and commercial/contract reviewers.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the matter is not already answered by a current source, addendum, prior response, or accessible tender document.
2. Assign a stable draft RFI ID and classify technical, commercial, contractual, administrative, design, quantity, schedule, or submission impact.
3. State the source requirement and problem accurately; avoid advocacy and unnecessary narrative.
4. Ask one clear question or a structured set of inseparable subquestions.
5. State why and when an answer is needed without threatening, claiming entitlement, committing price, or accepting risk.
6. Record affected requirement, scope, estimate, schedule, proposal, risk, assumption, and decision IDs.
7. Check duplication, confidentiality, attachments, form compliance, authorized contact, and issue deadline.
8. Mark every output as draft until authorized issue; record client response only from controlled evidence.

## Deliverables

- RFI register.
- Client-form RFI drafts.
- Duplicate and prior-answer report.
- Reviewer and issue-control sheet.
- Response impact and closure tracker.

## Completion gates

Require discipline, Tender Manager, and Commercial/Contracts review as applicable. The agent cannot issue the RFI.
