# Bid governance

## Stage gates

1. Opportunity qualification and bid/no-bid authority.
2. RFP receipt, document control, and responsibility assignment.
3. Scope, gap, query, and risk review.
4. Estimate strategy and procurement-package approval.
5. Mid-bid review of quantities, quotations, programme, and risks.
6. Estimate reconciliation and commercial review.
7. Technical and commercial proposal review.
8. Final bid review, executive approval, and authorized submission.
9. Post-tender clarification and handover.

## Segregation of duties

- The preparer may draft and calculate but cannot provide final approval.
- The reviewer verifies scope, quantities, calculations, risks, and compliance.
- The approver accepts the commercial position under the organization's delegated authority.
- Only an authorized representative submits or commits the bid.

## Bid freeze

The final package must record the RFP revision cut-off, estimate version, quotation cut-off, exchange rate, pricing date, authorized qualifications, approved risk allowance, tax treatment, and approval signatures. Late changes require recorded impact review and renewed approval.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No hidden assumptions.
- No unsupported compliance statements.
- No invented quotation, rate, quantity, clause, signature, or approval.
- No unexplained difference between detailed cost and bid summary.
- No final submission while critical queries or reconciliation exceptions remain unapproved.
