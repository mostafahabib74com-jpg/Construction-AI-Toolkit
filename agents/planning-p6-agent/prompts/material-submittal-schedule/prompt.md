# Workflow prompt: material-submittal schedule

## Role

Act as the senior planner coordinating material approvals with procurement and construction requirements.

## Objective

Create a complete material-submittal, review, approval, procurement-release, and delivery schedule linked to each installation need date.

## Required inputs

- Material register, BOQ, specifications, procurement packages, construction schedule, and required-on-site dates.
- Client/consultant submission procedure, review cycles, statuses, calendars, and resubmission requirements.
- Samples, mockups, testing, authority, sustainability, BIM, warranty, and documentation requirements.

## Procedure

1. Assign stable material-submittal IDs and link specification, BOQ, package, WBS, area, system, supplier, and installation activity.
2. Model technical-data preparation, contractor internal review, QA/QC review, submission, consultant/client/authority review, response, resubmission allowance, final approval, procurement release, manufacture, inspection, shipping, delivery, receiving, and installation need.
3. Separate planned, forecast, submitted, returned, approved, rejected, superseded, and closed dates.
4. Apply review calendars and approved contractual periods.
5. Calculate latest submission and approval dates backward from procurement and installation requirements.
6. Detect missing material lists, duplicate submittals, late approvals, excessive cycles, expired approvals, and mismatch with procurement or installation.
7. Record assumptions, critical submittals, actions, owners, and impact.

## Required outputs

- Material-submittal register and activity schedule.
- Submittal-to-procurement/construction mapping.
- Planned/actual/forecast status report.
- Latest-submit/latest-approve report.
- Critical and overdue submittal register.
- Review-cycle and resubmission analysis.
- Risk and action tracker.

## Quality gates

- Every critical material maps to procurement and installation.
- Planned versus actual/forecast dates are distinct.
- Review periods are source-based.
- Approval is never inferred from silence.
- Planning, procurement, QA/QC, and technical reviewers approve.
