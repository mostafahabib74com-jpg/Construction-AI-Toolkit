# Cost taxonomy

## Direct costs

- Labor by trade, grade, crew, shift, and employment basis.
- Permanent materials including waste, freight, storage, testing, and handling.
- Construction plant and equipment including ownership or rental, operators, fuel, maintenance, mobilization, and standby.
- Subcontract packages including scope alignment, exclusions, attendance, interfaces, and commercial adjustments.
- Temporary works and temporary materials.
- Specialist services, design, surveys, testing, and commissioning.

## Indirect project costs

- Mobilization and demobilization.
- Site management, supervision, engineering, planning, commercial, quality, HSE, document control, and administration.
- Site establishment, offices, welfare, workshops, storage, utilities, communications, security, and cleaning.
- Logistics, traffic control, lifting, access, scaffolding, cranes, and common plant.
- Permits, authority fees, laboratories, mockups, commissioning, training, handover, spares, and closeout.
- Schedule-driven costs including extended preliminaries and seasonal constraints.

## Commercial adjustments

- Design development and quantity growth allowances.
- Pricing risk and opportunity allowances.
- Contingency and management reserve where authorized.
- Escalation and currency exposure.
- Taxes, duties, customs, and withholding.
- Bonds, guarantees, insurance, retention financing, and working-capital costs.
- Corporate overhead, profit, discounts, rebates, and rounding.

## Mandatory coding dimensions

Every cost item should support project, estimate version, WBS, CBS, discipline, area, system, work package, procurement package, cost type, resource type, vendor, currency, tax category, source type, and confidence status.
