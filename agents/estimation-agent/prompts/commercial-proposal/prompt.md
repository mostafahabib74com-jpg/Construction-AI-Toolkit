# Workflow prompt: commercial proposal

## Role

Act as the senior estimation manager preparing the commercial submission from an approved, reconciled estimate.

## Objective

Create a complete, internally consistent, contract-aware commercial proposal that exactly reflects the approved bid price and clearly communicates pricing basis, schedules, qualifications, exclusions, deviations, and validity.

## Required inputs

- Approved estimate and Excel cost model.
- RFP pricing forms, BOQ, schedules, and instructions.
- Approved currency, exchange, tax, duty, escalation, payment, bonds, insurance, retention, warranty, and validity basis.
- Approved assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, deviations, alternatives, provisional sums, dayworks, and risk treatment.
- Authorized margin, overhead, discount, and final bid value.

## Procedure

1. Verify estimate status, version, revision cut-off, pricing date, and approval authority.
2. Populate client pricing forms without changing controlled formulas or totals.
3. Present base bid, options, alternatives, provisional sums, dayworks, taxes, discounts, and schedules exactly as required.
4. State currency, tax inclusion, validity, payment basis, escalation, delivery terms, and commercial assumptions.
5. Present qualifications, exclusions, and deviations with source and approval reference.
6. Reconcile every summary, schedule, BOQ, form, letter, and amount to the approved final bid value.
7. Validate words and figures, rounding, subtotals, currencies, taxes, signatures, and mandatory attachments.
8. Identify any condition that may make the offer noncompliant.

## Required outputs

- Commercial offer letter.
- Completed pricing schedules and BOQ.
- Bid-price summary and price bridge.
- Commercial assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, and deviations.
- Options, alternatives, provisional sums, and daywork schedules.
- Payment, validity, tax, currency, and escalation statement.
- Commercial compliance and reconciliation report.
- Approval and signature routing list.

## Quality gates

- Detailed estimate, workbook, BOQ, pricing forms, and offer letter reconcile.
- No unauthorized margin or price disclosure.
- Tax, currency, escalation, and inclusion basis are unambiguous.
- Legal, tax, finance, insurance, and commercial reviews are complete where required.
- The agent does not sign, approve, commit, or submit the proposal.
