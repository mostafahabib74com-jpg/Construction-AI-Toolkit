# RFP information taxonomy

RFP analysis shall classify requirements into the following controlled areas:

1. Procurement and submission instructions.
2. Project parties and governance.
3. Scope, boundaries, interfaces, and exclusions.
4. Design responsibility and design maturity.
5. Drawings, specifications, schedules, and BOQ.
6. Site data, surveys, access, utilities, and existing conditions.
7. Construction methodology, phasing, logistics, and temporary works.
8. Programme, milestones, sectional completion, and constraints.
9. Procurement, nominated suppliers, long-lead items, and free-issue materials.
10. Quality, inspections, testing, commissioning, and handover.
11. HSE, environmental, security, and permit requirements.
12. BIM, digital delivery, CDE, asset information, and data standards.
13. Commercial terms, pricing forms, currencies, taxes, duties, escalation, and payment.
14. Bonds, guarantees, insurance, retention, damages, and warranties.
15. Contract conditions, risk allocation, notices, variations, and claims.
16. Local content, workforce, localization, and authority requirements.
17. Bid evaluation criteria, alternatives, qualifications, and deviations.
18. Forms, signatures, file formats, portal rules, and deadlines.

Each requirement records its source, owner, response, cost impact, schedule impact, risk level, status, and inclusion in the final bid.
