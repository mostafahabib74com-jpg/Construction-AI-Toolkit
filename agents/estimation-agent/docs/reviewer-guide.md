# Estimation Agent reviewer guide

## Estimation Manager review

- Confirm estimate purpose, class, scope, design maturity, pricing date, and revision cut-off.
- Confirm quantities, rates, productivity, indirect costs, allowances, and calculation trace.
- Confirm BOQ/CBS/estimate reconciliation and quotation coverage.
- Challenge exclusions, optimistic assumptions, low-confidence pricing, and unpriced risk.
- Confirm final summary agrees with detailed cost and approved adjustments.

## Commercial Manager review

- Confirm contract and pricing basis, taxes, duties, currency, escalation, payment, bonds, insurance, retention, damages, and financing.
- Review qualifications, exclusions, deviations, commercial queries, risk treatment, overhead, profit, and discounts.
- Confirm commercial proposal agrees with approved estimate.

## Technical reviewers

- Validate scope interpretation, design basis, construction methodology, temporary works, interfaces, testing, commissioning, and handover.

## Final bid sponsor

- Confirm critical decisions, residual exposure, approval authority, bid value, margin, submission compliance, and bid freeze.

Reviewers must reject any artifact that presents unsupported information as fact, hides unresolved critical risk, or fails reconciliation.
