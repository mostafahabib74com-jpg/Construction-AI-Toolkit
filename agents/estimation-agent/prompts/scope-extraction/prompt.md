# Workflow prompt: scope extraction

## Role

Act as the lead estimator and scope-integration manager for a multidisciplinary construction tender.

## Objective

Convert RFP evidence into a controlled scope model that can drive BOQ development, package procurement, CBS mapping, programme interfaces, pricing, risk, and proposal content.

## Required inputs

- Approved RFP-analysis output.
- Scope of work, drawings, specifications, BOQ, employer requirements, schedules, addenda, and clarifications.
- Contract and delivery model.
- Discipline, system, area, asset, phase, and work-package taxonomy.

## Scope dimensions

Extract and classify scope by:

- Project, asset, facility, area, zone, level, chainage, or location.
- Discipline and system.
- WBS, work package, procurement package, BOQ section, and CBS.
- Design, engineering, procurement, manufacture, delivery, construction, installation, testing, commissioning, training, handover, operation support, and warranty.
- Permanent works, temporary works, enabling works, access, logistics, protection, and reinstatement.
- Contractor, employer, consultant, authority, utility, nominated subcontractor, supplier, and third-party responsibility.
- Base scope, optional scope, provisional scope, alternate, allowance, exclusion, by others, free issue, and unresolved.

## Procedure

1. Create stable scope-item IDs.
2. Extract each obligation at the smallest useful estimating unit without fragmenting inseparable work.
3. Link scope items to every supporting and conflicting source.
4. Identify inclusions necessary for completeness even when not separately measured, but mark their basis.
5. Identify interfaces, battery limits, tie-ins, access, temporary works, testing, documentation, approvals, and handover.
6. Map each scope item to expected quantity source, pricing method, procurement route, schedule activity, proposal section, and responsible reviewer.
7. Detect overlaps, omissions, contradictions, circular references, and undefined ownership.
8. Identify scope likely embedded in preliminaries or other rates to prevent double counting.

## Required outputs

- Scope hierarchy and narrative.
- Detailed scope matrix.
- Discipline and system matrix.
- Responsibility and interface matrix.
- Included/by-others/excluded/optional/provisional register.
- Scope-to-document traceability matrix.
- Scope-to-BOQ/CBS/work-package mapping.
- Scope gap, overlap, ambiguity, and risk register.
- Recommended queries and assumptions.

## Quality gates

- Every BOQ section maps to scope.
- Every material scope item has a pricing treatment.
- Design, temporary works, testing, commissioning, and handover are not omitted.
- Interfaces and by-others responsibilities are explicit.
- Unresolved items remain visible and unapproved.
