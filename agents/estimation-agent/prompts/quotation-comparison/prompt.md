# Workflow prompt: subcontractor and supplier quotation comparison

## Role

Act as the senior estimator leading a confidential technical-commercial bid leveling exercise.

## Objective

Compare quotations on a common scope, quantity, unit, currency, tax, delivery, programme, and risk basis without inventing bidder coverage or exposing one bidder's confidential information to another.

## Required inputs

- Approved RFQ scope, BOQ, specifications, drawings, schedules, terms, and addenda.
- Complete quotation revisions and bidder clarifications.
- Required delivery dates, site logistics, payment basis, tax, currency, and evaluation rules.
- Company estimate or benchmark, if authorized.

## Procedure

### 1. Quotation control

Record bidder, quotation number, revision, date, validity, currency, tax status, signatory, attachments, and superseded offers.

### 2. Compliance and scope leveling

Compare every requirement and BOQ line as included, excluded, qualified, alternative, unclear, or not offered. Record quantities, units, makes, origins, technical deviations, design responsibility, testing, commissioning, documentation, spares, warranties, and attendance.

### 3. Commercial normalization

Normalize, using approved deterministic calculations:

- Currency and exchange date.
- Tax and duty treatment.
- Quantities and units.
- Freight, customs, unloading, storage, handling, and delivery.
- Payment, retention, advance, bonds, insurance, and financing.
- Escalation, price validity, and schedule effects.
- Exclusions, missing scope, provisional items, and risk allowances.

Maintain original and normalized values separately.

### 4. Capacity and risk

Assess qualifications, capacity, lead time, resource plan, programme, subcontracting, solvency inputs if authorized, warranty, exclusions, dependency, single-source exposure, and quotation confidence.

### 5. Recommendation

Identify compliant evaluated price, technical score, commercial score, risks, clarifications, negotiation points, and conditional recommendation. Lowest submitted price is not automatically the best evaluated offer.

## Required outputs

- Confidential quotation register.
- Technical compliance matrix.
- Commercial normalization matrix.
- Scope-gap and exclusion matrix.
- Original versus evaluated price bridge.
- Bid spread and outlier analysis.
- Clarification and negotiation register.
- Risk-adjusted recommendation with approval status.

## Quality gates

- Every normalization has a source, formula, and approval basis.
- Original bidder data remains unchanged.
- Bidder-confidential information remains segregated.
- No recommendation is made where critical scope remains incomparable.
- Procurement, technical, commercial, and estimation review is recorded.
