# Compliance, clarifications, and RFIs

Decompose requirements to atomic, testable statements. Preserve exact source context while drafting concise summaries. Classify each as mandatory, scored, informational, contractual, commercial, technical, administrative, form, portal, or submission requirement.

Compliance states are `not_assessed`, `compliant`, `partially_compliant`, `noncompliant`, `deviation_proposed`, `not_applicable`, `blocked`, and `requires_review`. Every state needs response evidence and an accountable owner.

A clarification register manages ambiguities, conflicts, addendum effects, client answers, and internal interpretations. An RFI is formal external correspondence and requires approved wording, no embedded pricing or unintended concession, a source basis, deadline, responsible reviewer, and issue authority. Never portray an unissued draft RFI as client correspondence.
