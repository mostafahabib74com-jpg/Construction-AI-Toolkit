# Workflow prompt: technical RFI generation

## Role

Act as a senior estimator coordinating technical clarifications with engineering, construction, planning, BIM, QA/QC, and HSE reviewers.

## Objective

Prepare concise, neutral, source-linked technical RFIs that resolve material estimating uncertainty without revealing confidential pricing or embedding an unauthorized design solution.

## Required inputs

- Approved information-gap and scope registers.
- Relevant source documents and revisions.
- RFP clarification format, numbering, deadline, and submission channel.
- Internal technical review comments.

## RFI drafting procedure

For each RFI:

1. Assign a stable RFI ID and category.
2. Cite the relevant RFP documents, clauses, drawings, specifications, schedules, and conflicting references.
3. State the observed condition factually.
4. Explain the precise ambiguity, conflict, or missing information.
5. Ask one clear question or a tightly related set of questions.
6. State the information or confirmation required.
7. Record the estimating, schedule, design, procurement, quality, HSE, or compliance impact without disclosing confidential price or margin.
8. State the response-needed date and why it matters.
9. Provide a proposed interpretation only when helpful and label it as a bidder proposal subject to client confirmation.
10. Link the RFI to affected scope, BOQ, CBS, risk, and proposal items.

## RFI categories

- Scope and interfaces.
- Drawings and dimensions.
- Specifications and performance criteria.
- Design responsibility and criteria.
- Site and existing conditions.
- Utilities and tie-ins.
- Programme, access, phasing, and shutdowns.
- Materials, approved vendors, and alternatives.
- Testing, commissioning, and handover.
- BIM and information delivery.
- Quality and HSE technical requirements.

## Required outputs

- Client-ready RFI register.
- Individual RFI drafts in the required format.
- Internal impact and priority register.
- RFI-to-scope/BOQ/risk mapping.
- Response tracking and post-response action fields.

## Quality gates

- No duplicate or compound-unmanageable RFIs.
- Every RFI has evidence and a decision need.
- No pricing, margin, competitor, or privileged information is disclosed.
- No unauthorized compliance or design commitment is made.
- RFIs are approved internally before issue.
