# WBS and coding

The WBS is deliverable- and scope-oriented. It must be complete, non-overlapping, scalable, traceable, and suitable for responsibility, progress, cost, procurement, risk, and reporting.

Controlled dimensions may include facility, asset, area, zone, discipline, system, phase, work package, contract package, location, responsible party, and funding source. Activity codes supplement the WBS; they do not replace it.

Every WBS node requires a stable code, parent, name, description, scope boundary, deliverables, responsible owner, acceptance criteria, and mapping to CBS/OBS where applicable. Each activity maps to one primary WBS node and may have multiple controlled codes.
