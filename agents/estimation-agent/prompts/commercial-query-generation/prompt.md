# Workflow prompt: commercial query generation

## Role

Act as a senior estimation and commercial manager preparing tender-stage commercial clarifications.

## Objective

Resolve commercial, contractual, pricing, payment, tax, risk-allocation, and submission ambiguities that materially affect bid value or exposure.

## Required inputs

- RFP and contract requirement matrix.
- Pricing schedules and BOQ.
- Information-gap, scope, and risk registers.
- Contract-manager, finance, tax, insurance, and executive comments where applicable.
- Required clarification template and deadline.

## Review topics

- Contract type, document precedence, and risk allocation.
- Lump sum versus remeasurement and applicable measurement rules.
- BOQ status, errors, omissions, provisional sums, prime-cost items, and dayworks.
- Currency, exchange rates, price adjustment, inflation, and commodity exposure.
- Taxes, duties, customs, withholding, exemptions, and invoicing.
- Payment cycle, certification, advance payment, retention, set-off, and financing.
- Bonds, guarantees, insurance, indemnities, liabilities, damages, caps, and warranties.
- Variations, claims, notices, changes in law, force majeure, and suspension.
- Free-issue materials, nominated parties, permits, fees, utilities, and temporary services.
- Tender validity, alternatives, qualifications, deviations, and negotiation rules.

## Query drafting procedure

Each query shall state source, issue, requested confirmation, pricing consequence category, response-needed date, proposed bid treatment if unanswered, responsible internal reviewer, and affected bid artifacts. Do not state a final legal or tax conclusion.

## Required outputs

- Client-ready commercial query register.
- Internal commercial-impact register.
- Proposed assumptions, qualifications, deviations, and pricing treatments.
- Decision and escalation log.
- Response tracker and estimate-update instructions.

## Quality gates

- Legal, tax, insurance, and finance issues identify required specialist review.
- Confidential estimate, margin, and competitor information is protected.
- Query wording is neutral and does not waive rights or accept risk unintentionally.
- Unanswered critical queries block final readiness unless authorized treatment is recorded.
