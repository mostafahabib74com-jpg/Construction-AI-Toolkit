# Manpower histogram

## Objective

Produce a traceable manpower plan and histogram specification derived from approved resource-loaded schedule data or a controlled staffing plan.

## Required inputs

- Approved tender schedule version and resource assignments.
- Approved productivity, crew, shift, workweek, location, mobilization, and indirect-staff basis.
- Organization positions, equipment operators, subcontract strategy, and labor constraints.
- Estimation resource and cost artifacts where applicable.

## Procedure

1. Validate schedule version, calendars, data period, resource dictionary, assignment units, and source approval.
2. Map labor to trade/role, direct/indirect, company/subcontract, location, shift, activity, WBS, and period.
3. Calculate regular hours, overtime, average and peak headcount, mobilization, demobilization, and totals using deterministic tools.
4. Separate headcount from labor-hours and document conversion rules for each calendar/shift.
5. Reconcile key staff to the organization chart, operators to equipment, labor to methodology/work fronts, and resource cost to estimate where available.
6. Identify peaks, abrupt changes, insufficient supervision, labor availability constraints, accommodation/transport/welfare implications, and unsafe mobilization assumptions.
7. Define chart series, units, periods, stacking/grouping, totals, legends, source version, and accessibility text for rendering.

## Deliverables

- Periodized manpower data table.
- Manpower histogram rendering specification.
- Trade, role, direct/indirect, and location summaries.
- Peak and mobilization/demobilization analysis.
- Schedule-resource-organization reconciliation.
- Assumption and exception report.

## Completion gates

No histogram is valid without a deterministic calculation trace and Planning, Construction, Estimation/Commercial, HSE, and Tender review as applicable.
