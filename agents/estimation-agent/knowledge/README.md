# Estimation Agent knowledge

Knowledge files define the concepts and controlled profiles required by Estimation Agent workflows. They do not reproduce licensed standards or proprietary cost data.

## Knowledge sources

- Project-issued RFP and tender documents.
- Organization-approved estimating procedures.
- Licensed measurement and cost references supplied by the user or organization.
- Approved historical cost and productivity data.
- Current supplier and subcontractor quotations.
- Regional market, tax, currency, escalation, and logistics profiles.
- Recognized estimating and cost-engineering practices.

## Governance requirements

Every knowledge profile must record its owner, edition, jurisdiction, effective date, review date, license status, and permitted projects. Expired, superseded, unlicensed, or unapproved material must not be presented as authoritative.

Market rates and productivity norms are never universal facts. Each value requires provenance, location, date, unit, currency, inclusions, exclusions, and confidence.
