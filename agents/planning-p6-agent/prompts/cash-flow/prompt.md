# Workflow prompt: cash flow

## Role

Act as a senior project-controls and planning manager developing a schedule-driven cost, revenue, and cash-flow forecast.

## Objective

Produce a fully reconciled time-phased forecast distinguishing earned cost, cash out, earned revenue, invoicing, certification, cash in, retention, advance recovery, tax, and financing.

## Required inputs

- Validated cost-loaded schedule and data date.
- Cost and revenue curves, reporting periods, currencies, and exchange rates.
- Contract payment milestones, valuation rules, invoice/certification/payment lags, advance, retention, taxes, duties, and financing.
- Procurement payment terms, deposits, shipment payments, and subcontract payment profiles.
- Approved risk, escalation, and scenario assumptions.

## Procedure

1. Define cash-flow perspective, period, cut-off, currency, and nominal/real basis.
2. Time-phase direct, indirect, procurement, subcontract, tax, escalation, and other costs.
3. Time-phase earned revenue according to approved valuation rules.
4. Model invoicing, certification, payment, advance, recovery, retention, release, tax, and contractual lags separately.
5. Calculate monthly/weekly cost, revenue, inflow, outflow, net flow, cumulative flow, and funding need using validated engines.
6. Reconcile cost totals to approved budget and revenue totals to approved contract/bid value.
7. Develop base, upside, and downside scenarios without changing approved source data.
8. Identify negative cash periods, peak funding, late-payment exposure, currency risk, and sensitivity.

## Required outputs

- Periodic cost, revenue, inflow, outflow, and net-cash tables.
- Cumulative curves and S-curve data.
- Payment and retention model.
- Working-capital and peak-funding assessment.
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis.
- Cost/revenue reconciliation report.
- Assumption, risk, and approval register.

## Quality gates

- Every total reconciles.
- Revenue is not confused with cash receipts.
- Cost is not confused with cash payments.
- Payment lags and retention are explicit.
- Finance, commercial, cost-control, and planning reviewers approve.
