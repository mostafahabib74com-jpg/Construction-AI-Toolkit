# Proposal management

Build the proposal around the client's evaluation logic, required response structure, page limits, forms, and evidence—not a generic marketing narrative. A compliant answer addresses the requirement, states the proposed solution, explains delivery and controls, provides verified evidence, identifies benefits or differentiators, and cross-references supporting schedules.

Use approved win themes that are specific, provable, relevant, and consistently supported. Remove unsupported superlatives, invented project references, unavailable personnel, unverified certifications, and confidential information from other clients or tenders.

Maintain section owners, contributors, planned and actual dates, content status, evidence status, review comments, version, and final disposition.
