# Technical proposal outline

Use the client-prescribed structure when supplied. This outline is a controlled fallback and tailoring checklist.

1. Cover, document control, confidentiality, and authorized letter.
2. Executive summary, understanding, response commitments, and verified win themes.
3. Requirement-by-requirement compliance cross-reference.
4. Project understanding, scope, boundaries, interfaces, constraints, and assumptions.
5. Delivery strategy and execution methodology.
6. Mobilization, site establishment, logistics, access, traffic, lifting, storage, and temporary facilities.
7. Design management, design responsibility, BIM/digital delivery, coordination, and document control.
8. Procurement, subcontracting, material submittals, shop drawings, manufacture, logistics, and long-lead control.
9. Construction sequence by phase, area, discipline, system, and work package.
10. Project schedule, milestones, calendars, critical controls, resources, and recovery approach.
11. Organization, governance, roles, RACI, key personnel, and stakeholder interfaces.
12. Equipment schedule, manpower plan, and mobilization/demobilization.
13. Project controls: schedule, cost, risk, change, reporting, information, and meetings.
14. QA/QC plan, ITPs, inspections, testing, NCRs, materials, and closeout.
15. HSE, environmental, security, emergency, welfare, and permit controls.
16. Testing, commissioning, performance demonstration, training, handover, and defects support.
17. Sustainability, localization, social value, innovation, and value engineering where required.
18. Risk, opportunity, contingency, assumptions, qualifications, deviations, and exclusions cross-reference.
19. Verified company experience, capabilities, certifications, references, and appendices.
20. Forms, schedules, declarations, and evidence required in the technical envelope.

Each section records requirement IDs, owner, approved source artifacts, evidence, version, reviewer, page limit, status, and final cross-reference.
