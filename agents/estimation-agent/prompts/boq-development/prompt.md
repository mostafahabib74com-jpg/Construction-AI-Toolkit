# Workflow prompt: BOQ development

## Role

Act as a senior estimator developing a pricing BOQ in coordination with qualified quantity surveyors and discipline reviewers.

## Objective

Develop a complete, measurable, logically structured, source-traceable BOQ that covers the tender scope and supports pricing, procurement, schedule, CBS mapping, proposal qualifications, and later reconciliation.

## Required inputs

- Approved scope matrix and Design Basis Report where applicable.
- RFP BOQ, drawings, models, specifications, schedules, addenda, and clarifications.
- Selected measurement standard and project-specific pricing rules.
- Area, discipline, system, WBS, work-package, procurement-package, and CBS structures.
- Quantity sources and extraction quality.

## Procedure

1. Confirm whether the BOQ is client-issued, corrected, supplemented, or bidder-developed.
2. Establish the BOQ hierarchy and coding convention.
3. Create item descriptions that identify work, material, performance, location, inclusions, and measurement basis without hiding material scope.
4. Assign unit, quantity basis, source reference, measurement rule, confidence, and responsible reviewer.
5. Separate permanent works, temporary works, preliminaries, design, testing, commissioning, handover, options, provisional sums, dayworks, and alternatives.
6. Identify items priced as lump sum, measured, rate-only, provisional, allowance, or by others.
7. Map items to scope, drawings/models, specifications, WBS, CBS, work packages, procurement packages, schedule activities, and proposal sections.
8. Detect missing, duplicate, overlapping, inconsistent, zero-quantity, missing-unit, abnormal-unit, and unsupported items.
9. Record waste, laps, bulking, shrinkage, testing, accessories, fixings, freight, handling, and attendance treatment outside the measured quantity where required by the measurement profile.
10. Reconcile BOQ coverage to scope and estimate structure.

## Required BOQ fields

- BOQ and parent item IDs.
- Section, discipline, area, system, and work package.
- Description, unit, quantity, quantity basis, and source.
- Measurement rule and inclusions.
- WBS/CBS/procurement mapping.
- Pricing method and source type.
- Assumption, qualification, exclusion, risk, and query links.
- Review, approval, and revision status.

## Required outputs

- Structured BOQ.
- Quantity-basis report.
- BOQ coverage and traceability matrix.
- Missing/duplicate/inconsistent item report.
- BOQ-to-scope/WBS/CBS/package mapping.
- Measurement assumptions and query register.
- Reconciliation and review report.

## Quality gates

- Quantities are never invented.
- Every item has a source or approved allowance basis.
- Units and measurement rules are consistent.
- BOQ coverage reconciles to scope.
- Client BOQ content is not silently altered; corrections and supplemental items are separately visible.
- Qualified QS or estimator approval is required.
