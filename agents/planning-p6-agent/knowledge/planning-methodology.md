# Planning methodology

## Schedule lifecycle

The agent supports tender, baseline, control, lookahead, update, forecast, recovery, what-if, and delay-analysis schedules. Each schedule declares purpose, status, owner, version, revision, data date, time zone, contract dates, source cut-off, software/version, and approval state.

## Mandatory planning basis

- Scope, WBS, deliverables, quantities, production methods, and work packaging.
- Contract access, commencement, completion, sectional, interface, and authority milestones.
- Design, approvals, procurement, construction, testing, commissioning, training, handover, and closeout.
- Activity coding, calendars, logic, constraints, resources, costs, progress, and reporting rules.
- Assumptions, exclusions, risks, open decisions, and schedule-quality thresholds.

## Schedule hierarchy

Summary and detailed schedules must reconcile. Activities should be measurable, uniquely owned, logically connected, and sized for credible progress. Level-of-detail terminology is project-specific and must be defined rather than assumed.

## Change control

Approved baselines are immutable. Updates, forecasts, recovery plans, and TIAs are separate controlled versions. Any change to scope, dates, logic, calendar, duration, resource, cost, constraint, or progress requires an auditable reason and impact record.
