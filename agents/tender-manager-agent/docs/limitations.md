# Limitations

- The specification does not parse PDFs, Office files, images, XER/XML, BIM files, or portal pages.
- It does not generate final Word, Excel, PDF, chart, ZIP, or native schedule files without validated rendering and calculation services.
- It cannot verify facts merely because they appear in generated text.
- It cannot determine legal compliance, contract acceptability, or bid/no-bid approval autonomously.
- It cannot sign forms, commit price, issue correspondence, authorize submission, or upload files.
- It cannot guarantee portal availability, upload duration, antivirus behavior, or client receipt.
