# Technical proposal

## Objective

Assemble a compliant, persuasive, evidence-based technical proposal from approved discipline contributions and verified company content.

## Required inputs

- Approved compliance matrix and evaluation criteria.
- Client-required structure, language, page limits, formats, and forms.
- Approved methodology, organization, equipment, manpower, schedule, procurement, BIM, QA/QC, HSE, sustainability, risk, commissioning, and handover artifacts.
- Verified company profile, experience, certifications, personnel, and references.
- Approved assumptions, exclusions, qualifications, deviations, and clarifications relevant to technical content.

## Procedure

1. Build a response outline mapped one-to-one to requirements and scoring criteria.
2. For each section state compliance, proposed solution, delivery method, governance and controls, interfaces, evidence, benefits, risks, and cross-references.
3. Preserve approved facts and versions from owning contributors; never invent credentials, performance, availability, dates, quantities, or commitments.
4. Integrate methodology, logistics, program, resources, procurement, design/BIM, QA/QC, HSE, environment, testing, commissioning, handover, digital delivery, and project controls consistently.
5. Use approved win themes only where supported by specific evidence.
6. Check requirements coverage, terminology, numbers, dates, roles, figures, captions, references, accessibility, page limits, and conflict with commercial content.
7. Produce section-level comment and approval status; block final assembly for unapproved mandatory content.

## Deliverables

- Technical proposal outline and response matrix.
- Draft technical proposal content.
- Figure, table, and evidence schedule.
- Section ownership and review tracker.
- Technical compliance and consistency report.
- Final rendering specification.

## Completion gates

Every applicable discipline owner approves the exact content and artifact version. Tender Manager and Technical Manager complete final integration review.
